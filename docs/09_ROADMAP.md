- domain registry checks and registrations;
- brand asset placeholders;
- repository created.

## Where this stands (13 August 2026)

Phases 1 and 3 to 8 are complete and verified on real WordPress installations.
Phase 2 is complete except for its last line, **submit FREE**, which is held
back deliberately: nothing is published under this name until Phase 0's manual
trademark similarity check is done, and the family submits together.

Phase 0's remaining item is therefore the only thing blocking release of either
plugin, and it is a decision about the OxyWP family rather than about OxyArea:
Soflyy asks that "oxy" not appear in product names.
